What's new in Minutes 0.1.14

  • The recorder now drops down from the menu bar. Drag it off the menu bar to keep it on screen while you record, and drop it back to put it away.
  • Press Option-Command-R in any app to open the recorder. In Settings, under Recorder, you can change the shortcut and choose a compact layout.
  • The recorder shows your next calendar meeting. Click the meeting to name the recording after it, or click Join to also open the call.
  • The Minutes window is now your library of past meetings. Meeting lengths show as durations, such as 1 h 6 min.
  • Minutes tells you when a transcript is ready.
